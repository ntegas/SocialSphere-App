# Privacy Policy — SocialSphere

_Last updated: 2026-10-28_ · [Ελληνικά](PRIVACY_POLICY_EL.md)

SocialSphere is a personal, offline-first relationship manager for your own contacts,
events and reminders. It is built privacy-first: it has no server of its own, no
accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## What data the app holds and where it is stored
Contacts, companies, events, notes, gift ideas, addresses, tags, contact photos and
settings are **stored only on your device**, in the app's database. The developer
**does not collect, receive or have access to** this data. Android's automatic cloud
backup is **turned off** for the app, so the system does not upload your data to the
cloud.

## Permissions the app asks for, and why
Each permission is requested when you use the related feature and is used only for it.

- **Contacts (read)** — importing contacts from your phone book when you ask. The app
  never writes to your phone book itself: when you export "to phone", a vCard file
  opens in the Contacts app and you are the one who saves it.
- **Camera** — business-card scanner. The photo is processed **on the device** (text
  recognition), kept only in memory while it is recognised, not sent anywhere and not
  saved to your gallery.
- **Microphone** — voice input. Only **on-device speech recognition** is used
  (Android 12 and newer). Audio is not sent over the network. If your device has no
  built-in recogniser, the feature is unavailable.
- **Location** — to show you on the map and to build a route to a contact or company.
  We do not store or transmit your coordinates.
- **Calendar (read and write)** — only on your command: a one-way export of the app's
  events to your phone's calendar. Reading is used to avoid duplicating events that
  were already exported.
- **Notifications, exact alarms, start after reboot** — local reminders (birthdays,
  "time to get in touch", events). Scheduled on the device, no network.
- **Biometrics / device credential** — unlocking the app with the system prompt. The
  app only receives "confirmed / not confirmed"; it never has access to biometric data.
- **Internet and network state** — only for the map and for finding coordinates of an
  address (see below), and for opening links.

## App PIN
If you set a PIN, only a salted, irreversible hash of it is stored on the device. The
PIN itself is never stored.

## What can leave the device
Only what these features cannot work without, and only when you use them:
- **Google Maps** — to display the map the app talks to Google's service; Google
  receives technical map-request data (for example your IP address) under Google's own
  privacy policy.
- **Address geocoding** — an address from a contact card is passed to Android's
  system geocoding service (on most devices, Google's) to obtain coordinates. The app
  itself sends this data nowhere else.
- **Reaching a contact** — when you tap Call, Message, Email or a messenger link, the
  corresponding app opens and handles the data from then on.
- **Export files and backups** (vCard, CSV, JSON) are created when you ask and are
  saved where you choose, or passed through the system Share menu to the app you
  pick. These files are not encrypted — please keep them safe.

Your data is not shared with third parties, not sold, and not used for advertising.

## Retention and deletion
Data stays until you delete it. **Settings → Privacy** has a "Delete all data" action.
Uninstalling the app erases all local data.

## Children
The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data
about them.

## Changes
If this policy changes (for example, when new features are added), a new version with
a new date will be published here.
